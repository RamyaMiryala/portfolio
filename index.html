About

Ramya Miryala is the Director and Chief Operating Officer of the Deaf Enabled Foundation (DEF), a nonprofit that empowers the Deaf community in India.

Her work and the foundation's programmes have been covered by outlets including The Hindu, BBC, The Better India, YourStory and Namasthe Telangana. The coverage is collected below.
